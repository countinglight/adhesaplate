Close tolerance is guaranteed on a wide variety of standard or custom shapes and material thicknesses. We can provide kiss cut / strip form labels to accurate .060 thick custom panels. Note that rectangular shapes not requiring close tolerance can be cut without a die tool and rounded corners are available at minimal cost. Laser cutting is also available.

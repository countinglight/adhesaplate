Our laminating process provides laminations for protection and/or high performance adhesive for special adhesion issues. High gloss and matte mylars create a solvent and scratch resistant barrier to protect the printed image. We exclusively use 3M adhesives, applying the adhesive that is best suited for your specific needs. For zoned adhesive requirements, a 3M sheet adhesive can be custom cut and applied to create custom selective adhesive coverage.

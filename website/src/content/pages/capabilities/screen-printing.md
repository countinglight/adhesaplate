Screen printing provides durability to meet outdoor requirements. With UV technology, print quality is unsurpassed. Accurate color matching and opacity of inks makes this process versatile for a variety of products, including membrane switch or backlit graphic overlays. Special effects and metallic colors are available. Other print styles cannot match the luster on the special materials used for label applications. Zoned textured hard coatings and 3M 467 printable adhesives are also available using the screen printing process.

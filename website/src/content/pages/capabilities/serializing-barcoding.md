We can apply sequential human readable numbers, static & sequential barcodes, as well as UID codes to various label materials for a number of different applications. Using Adhesa Plates' digital printing technology, alpha-numeric sequences, custom font sizes and number of digits is unrestrictive.
Applications include: warranty labels, asset labels, date of manufacture labels, parking permits, DOD labels and model/serial nameplates.
For debossed sequential numbers into foil labels, numbers are available in 1/8", 3/16", and 1/4" heights and 6 digits sequences.

## UV InkJet Printing

Digital UV inkjet printing offers the highest quality image using CMYK 4 color process with outdoor durability. This option enables you to use unlimited colors for the cost of 4 colors. Like thermal transfer, inkjet printing allows variable data, eg. serial numbers (either bar code or human readable) of any font size or number of digits. Compare to thermal transfer, UV inkjet printing does not have material restrictions. This process has the ability to print on customer supplied parts. Our latest UV inkjet printer/cutter also has the capabilities to prints CMYK + White + Clear for unmatched color, texture and dimension. Clear coat adds remarkably rich special effects ranging from high-gloss finish for area highlights to custom-textured effects.

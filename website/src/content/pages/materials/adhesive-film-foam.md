## Adhesive, Film, & Foam

Besides the typical graphic overlays or product markings, Adhesa Plate also manufactures other adhesive backed products. We have a variety of material, adhesive, film, and foam for creating spacers, insulators & gaskets for many special applications. These custom products can be use to add shock absorption and support for internal components, hold down wiring cables to reduce vibration or possible disconnection, shielding and insulating of close fitting components to guard against interference, and display lenses.

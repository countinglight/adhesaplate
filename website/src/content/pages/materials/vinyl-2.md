UL 969 approved; Adhesaflex-Ve
Use: Indoor or outdoor. Decals, warning signs, instruction labels, product identification, transferable labels, destructible labels, window permits or window clings.
Qualities: Permanent, removable, and LSE adhesive available for most surfaces including powder coated surfaces. Weather resistant. Flexible and inexpensive.
Temperature: Resistant up to 176°F.
Thickness: .004
Color: White, lemon yellow, safety yellow, black, red and matte clear.
Finish: Semi-gloss

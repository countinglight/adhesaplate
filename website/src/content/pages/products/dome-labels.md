Custom dome or domed urethane coated labels are made from white, clear, chrome silver and brushed silver polyester label materials. A .060” durable, clear, non-yellowing urethane coating adds a high end 3-D look to “Identify your Product”. Outdoor durable which can self-repair any dents that occur. Available with an LSE adhesive for powder coated surfaces.

#### Application:

- Product ID labels for appliances, computers, & tools.

- Equipment labels

- Electronic device labels

- Special promotion decals

#### Features:

- The thick clear polyurethane coating creates attractive high-end look and 3-D effect

- Polyurethane coating can 'self-heal' if indented

- Printing transparent ink on shiny material and adding dome coating on top can creates very eye-catching labels

- All purpose LSE adhesive that will adhere to powder coated paint and polyethylene

#### Specifications:

- Digitally printed with full color option or Screen printed for precise color-matching

- Available on 2 mil white, clear, shiny chrome silver & brushed silver polyester

- Custom shapes & sizes

- Domed coating is non-yellowing and suitable for both indoor & outdoor use

These custom product ID & device labels utilizes the benefits of UV ink jet printing & digital cutting which eliminated the cost for film & die tool. The colorful image and custom shape is furthermore enhanced by the urethane dome coating. These custom labels are produced on 2 mil polyester material with 3M’s hi performance adhesive for durability.
